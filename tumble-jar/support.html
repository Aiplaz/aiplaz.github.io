Questions, bugs, ideas

Write to support.aiplaz@gmail.com. For a bug, please add your phone model, the Android or iOS version and what happened just before. We usually answer within a few days. You can write in English or Romanian.

How do I play?

Put your finger anywhere on the jar, slide sideways to aim and lift your finger to throw. A quick tap drops the ball straight down. Two equal balls merge into the next number. The water keeps rising; a merge that touches the water boils some of it off. The game ends when the water reaches the red line or a ball stays above it.

Why did the water jump up?

Tapping too fast, right after the last ball, drops nothing and raises the water (“Too fast!”). Not throwing for a while makes the water rise quickly towards the red line. Keep a steady pace.

Sound and vibration

Turn them on or off in Settings → Sound and vibration.

Can I see the first game again?

Yes. Open Settings and tap How to play, under Help and privacy: it replays the guided first game.

I lost my best score

Your best score and statistics are saved only on your phone, so they are gone if the game is uninstalled or its data is cleared, and they do not move to a new phone. There are no accounts, so they cannot be restored.

Statistics

The game sends anonymous statistics and crash reports (PostHog). Turn both off in Settings → Anonymous statistics. Details in the privacy policy.

Română

Scrie-ne la support.aiplaz@gmail.com, cu modelul telefonului și versiunea de Android sau iOS. Pune degetul pe borcan, trage în lateral ca să ochești și ridică degetul ca să arunci; o atingere scurtă lasă bila să cadă drept. Dacă atingi prea repede sau nu arunci o vreme, apa crește. Sunetele și vibrațiile le oprești din Setări → Sunet și vibrații, iar Setări → Cum se joacă reia partida ghidată. Recordul și statisticile se păstrează doar pe telefon și nu se pot recupera după dezinstalare. Statisticile anonime se opresc din Setări → Statistici anonime.
