Tumble Jar is a physics merge game. Throw numbered balls into a jar that fills the whole screen: two equal balls merge into the next number, from 2 all the way up to 4096. The water keeps rising, and only a merge that touches it boils some away, so aim well before the jar floods.

  • 2 One fingerTouch the jar, slide sideways to aim and lift your finger to throw. A quick tap drops the ball straight down.
  • 4 Equal balls merge2 and 2 make 4, 4 and 4 make 8, and so on up to 4096.
  • 8 The water keeps risingA merge in the water boils some of it off. Merge fast enough in the water and it starts to boil, for double points.
  • 16 Bombs and stonesBombs clear small balls. Drop too fast in one spot and the ball turns into a stone that never goes away.
  • 32 Levels that get harderThe higher your score, the wilder the jar: calm, then bombs, waves, big balls and storms.
  • 64 One mode: EndlessPlay until the jar is full and beat your best score. Your statistics stay on your phone.
  • 128 13 languages, no accountPlays offline. No account needed.
