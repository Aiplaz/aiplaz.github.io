Delete your data

Tumble Jar by Aiplaz

Tumble Jar has no accounts. This is how to delete each kind of data the game keeps or sends:

1. Settings and statistics on your phone

Your settings, best score and the other statistics are stored only on your phone. Uninstall the game, or clear its storage in the system settings, and they are deleted. To delete only the statistics, open Statistics in the game and tap Reset statistics.

2. Stop sending statistics and crash reports

In the game open Settings → Anonymous statistics and turn it off. From then on the game sends no statistics (PostHog) and no crash reports (Sentry).

3. Delete what was already sent

Write to support.aiplaz@gmail.com with the subject “Tumble Jar data deletion”. Because the data is anonymous, please add your phone model and roughly when you installed the game, so the matching statistics and crash reports can be found. They are deleted within 30 days and you get a reply when it is done. Nothing is kept afterwards, except what the law requires.

Română

Setările și statisticile stau doar pe telefon și se șterg când dezinstalezi jocul (doar statisticile le poți șterge și din Statistici → Resetează statisticile). Statisticile anonime și rapoartele de erori le oprești din Setări → Statistici anonime. Ca să ștergi ce s-a trimis deja, scrie la support.aiplaz@gmail.com cu modelul telefonului și cam când ai instalat jocul; datele se șterg în cel mult 30 de zile.
