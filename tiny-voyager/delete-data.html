Delete your data

Tiny Voyager by Nicolae Sirghi

Tiny Voyager has no accounts. This is how to delete each kind of data the game keeps or sends:

1. Progress on your phone

Levels, stars, characters, boosts, settings and ghosts are stored only on your phone. Uninstall the game, or clear its storage in the system settings, and they are deleted.

2. Stop sending statistics and crash reports

In the game open Settings → Anonymous statistics and turn it off. From then on the game sends no statistics and no crash reports (PostHog; Sentry in versions up to 1.1.1).

3. Delete what was already sent

Write to tinyvoyager.games@gmail.com with the subject “Tiny Voyager data deletion”. Because the data is anonymous, please add your phone model and roughly when you installed the game, so the matching statistics and crash reports can be found. They are deleted within 30 days and you get a reply when it is done. Nothing is kept afterwards, except what the law requires.

Data used for ads is held by Google, not by the game: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings and manage it at myadcenter.google.com.

Română

Progresul stă doar pe telefon și se șterge când dezinstalezi jocul. Statisticile și rapoartele de erori le oprești din Setări → Statistici anonime. Ca să ștergi ce s-a trimis deja, scrie la tinyvoyager.games@gmail.com cu modelul telefonului și cam când ai instalat jocul; datele se șterg în cel mult 30 de zile.
