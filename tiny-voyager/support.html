Support

Questions, bugs, ideas

Write to tinyvoyager.games@gmail.com. For a bug, please add your phone model, the system version and what happened just before. I usually answer within a few days. You can write in English or Romanian.

How do I play?

Hold anywhere on the screen to fly right, let go to fly left. Collect stars, dodge everything else. The help screen (the "?" button in the menu) shows every world and what lives there.

I lost my progress

Progress is saved only on your phone, so it is gone if the game is uninstalled or its data is cleared, and it does not move to a new phone. There are no accounts, so it cannot be restored.

Ads

Ads appear only when you tap a button to watch one for a reward. If no ad is available right now, the button does not appear; try again later. In Europe you can change your ad consent in Settings → Ad privacy choices.

Statistics

The game sends anonymous statistics and crash reports (PostHog). Turn both off in Settings → Anonymous statistics. Details in the privacy policy.

Română

Scrie-mi la tinyvoyager.games@gmail.com. Progresul se păstrează doar pe telefon și nu se poate recupera după dezinstalare. Reclamele apar doar când alegi să vezi una. Statisticile se opresc din Setări → Statistici anonime. Detalii în politica de confidențialitate.
