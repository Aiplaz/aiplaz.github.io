Delete your data

Flip Troupe by Aiplaz

Flip Troupe has no accounts. This is how to delete each kind of data the game keeps or sends:

1. Progress and settings on your phone

Uninstall the game, or open your phone's Settings → Apps → Flip Troupe → Storage → Clear storage. To delete only the progress, open Settings in the game and tap Reset progress.

2. Stop sending statistics and error reports

In the game open Settings → Anonymous statistics and turn it off. From then on nothing is sent.

3. Delete what was already sent

Write to support.aiplaz@gmail.com with the subject “Flip Troupe data deletion”. Because the data is anonymous, please add your phone model and roughly when you installed the game. It is deleted within 30 days and you get a reply when it is done.

Română

Progresul și setările stau doar pe telefon și se șterg când dezinstalezi jocul. Statisticile anonime și rapoartele de erori le oprești din Setări → Statistici anonime. Ca să ștergi ce s-a trimis deja, scrie la support.aiplaz@gmail.com cu modelul telefonului și cam când ai instalat jocul; datele se șterg în cel mult 30 de zile.
